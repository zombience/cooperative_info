# Questions for a financial professional

The plan is to self-educate first and then hire a financial professional (a CPA familiar with co-ops, a co-op development consultant, or a fee-only planner) to **review** the decisions near the end. This is the list to bring to them. Prefer someone paid a flat or hourly fee rather than on commission.

## The co-op's numbers
- [ ] Does our pro forma budget (income, carrying charges, expenses, reserves) hold up? What did we underestimate?
- [ ] Are our replacement and operating reserves sized sensibly for this building's age and condition?
- [ ] Given lender requirements (LTV, DSCR), how much equity do we really need at closing?
- [ ] How should we treat the two later members' shares until they join, financially and on the books?
- [ ] Is our resale formula (if limited equity) fair to both exiting and incoming members over 5, 10, and 20 years?
- [ ] Can the co-op absorb one member leaving suddenly? Model the cash flow.

## Tax
- [ ] Will we qualify for Section 216 treatment? What has to be true every year to keep it?
- [ ] How will each member's mortgage interest and property tax deduction be reported (Form 1098 or statement from the co-op)?
- [ ] Any tax consequences for member loans, or for members paying different amounts?

## Each member's household
- [ ] For each of us: how does this buy-in compare to renting and investing the difference, in our specific situation?
- [ ] How should we each think about liquidity, given shares may take time to sell?

## Ongoing
- [ ] What bookkeeping system and annual review should we set up?
- [ ] Do we need an annual audit or review, or just a compiled statement?
