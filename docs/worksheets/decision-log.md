# Decision log

A running record of what the group decided, when, and why. Future members (including the two joining later) will rely on this to understand how things got the way they are.

Copy the template below for each decision. Newest at the top.

---

### Template

- **Decision:** _one sentence_
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Who was present / voted:**
- **Options considered:**
    1.
    2.
- **What we chose and why:**
- **Open questions / revisit by:**
- **Links:** _to notes, emails, lawyer memos_
