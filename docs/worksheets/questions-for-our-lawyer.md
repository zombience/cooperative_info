# Questions for our lawyer

Our co-op attorney handles the legal structure. This list is so we show up prepared and make the most of billable time. Check items off, and record answers in the [decision log](decision-log.md).

## Entity and structure
- [ ] Which entity type do you recommend for a 3-household co-op (three couples) in Illinois: a cooperative corporation, a not-for-profit corporation, an LLC, or something else? Why, for our size?
- [ ] What would we gain or lose by being a "true" housing cooperative for tax purposes (IRC § 216) versus an LLC?
- [ ] Which Illinois statute will we be organized under, and what does it require of us (board, annual meetings, filings)?

## Members joining and leaving
- [ ] How do we legally reserve a membership for the couple who will join in about two years? Treasury shares, a subscription agreement with a future closing date, an option, or something else?
- [ ] Can the founding members lend money to the co-op (member loans) that is repaid when the later members buy in? How should those notes be documented?
- [ ] How should the bylaws handle a member who wants to leave, a member who can't pay, divorce or breakup, death, or a member who wants to rent out their unit?
- [ ] What approval process can we legally require for new members, and what fair housing rules apply?
- [ ] If we choose limited equity, how do we write the resale formula so it's enforceable and can't easily be voted away?

## Property, taxes, transfers
- [ ] Does Chicago or Cook County transfer tax apply when a co-op share changes hands? Who pays?
- [ ] How do homestead exemptions work for co-op residents in Cook County, and what paperwork does the co-op file each year?
- [ ] If we rent a unit to non-members (the third unit until the later couple joins, or a fourth unit in a 4-flat long-term), how does that affect co-op status, Section 216, and landlord-tenant obligations?
- [ ] Is a membership held by a couple jointly? What happens to it if the couple separates?

## Financing
- [ ] Will a lender require personal guarantees from members? What are we each exposed to if the co-op defaults?
- [ ] Do the governing documents need specific language to satisfy lenders like NCB or a CDFI?

## Process
- [ ] What's the order of operations (form entity, open bank account, collect capital, sign purchase contract, close)?
- [ ] What should we decide among ourselves before your next drafting step?
