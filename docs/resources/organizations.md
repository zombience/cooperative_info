# Organizations

Who to know, and what they're good for. For Chicago-specific groups, see [Chicago and Illinois](chicago-illinois.md#local-organizations-lenders-and-programs).

| Organization | What it's good for |
|---|---|
| [Center for Shared Ownership](https://cclfchicago.org/center-for-shared-ownership/) (Chicago) | Local training and technical help for co-ops. **Contact early.** |
| [Chicago Community Loan Fund](https://cclfchicago.org/) | A local lender to co-ops. |
| [Pilsen Housing Cooperative](https://pilsenhousingcoop.org/) | Peers who bought a 6-unit building with member capital plus a co-op loan. |
| [Shared Capital Cooperative](https://sharedcapital.coop/) | A national lender for co-ops; financed Pilsen's purchase. |
| [National Association of Housing Cooperatives (NAHC)](https://coophousing.org/) | The US trade association for housing co-ops: [publications](https://coophousing.org/publications/), education, and conferences. |
| [National Cooperative Bank](https://ncb.coop/sectors-we-serve/housing) | Co-op mortgages and share loans. |
| [UHAB](https://www.uhab.org/) | NYC-focused, but its [resale policy guide](https://uhab.org/resource/guide-to-resale-policies/) is the best template we found. |
| [Sustainable Economies Law Center](https://www.theselc.org/) / [Co-op Law](https://www.co-oplaw.org/) | Legal education for co-ops, including the [Illinois law survey](https://co-oplaw.org/illinois-cooperative-law/) and the Permanent Real Estate Cooperative model. |
| [Cooperative Housing International](https://www.housinginternational.coop/) | The global co-op housing network, with an explainer on starting a co-op. |
| [Grounded Solutions Network](https://groundedsolutions.org/strengthening-neighborhoods/shared-equity-homeownership) | National hub for shared-equity homeownership (permanently affordable ownership) and community land trusts. |
| [Foundation for Intentional Community](https://www.ic.org/) | Free guides on legal structures and buying property as a group. |
| [Supernuclear](https://supernuclear.substack.com/) | Friend co-living and co-buying stories. See [Radish](radish-and-cobuying.md). |
