# Co-op finance and ownership structures

The core of our self-education. Tags: 🟢 beginner, 🟡 intermediate, 🔴 advanced. Links checked 2026-10-04.

## Understanding the models
| | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | [What is Cooperative Housing?](https://coophousing.org/what-is-cooperative-housing/) (NAHC) | A short national primer on market-rate, limited-equity and senior co-ops. |
| 🟢 | [Types of housing co-ops and other shared housing models](https://cdi.coop/coop-cathy-types-of-housing-coops/) (Cooperative Development Institute) | Compares market-rate, limited-equity and group-equity co-ops, and explains why democratic control is what makes something a co-op. |
| 🟢 | [Housing Cooperatives: A Cooperative Approach](https://resources.uwcc.wisc.edu/About%20Co-ops/Housing_Cooperatives_online.pdf) (UW–Madison Center for Cooperatives, PDF) | Explains the equity models, blanket loans and monthly occupancy charges, with Midwest case studies. |
| 🟢 | [Three major types of resident-controlled housing](http://shelterforce.org/2024/01/04/q-what-are-the-three-major-types-of-community-or-resident-controlled-housing-and-how-do-they-work/) (Shelterforce, 2024) | Co-ops vs. community land trusts vs. mutual housing. The clearest quick comparison. |
| 🟢 | [Limited Equity Cooperatives](https://www.housingsolutionslab.org/publication/limited-equity-cooperatives/) (NYU Furman Center) | How limited-equity co-ops work and how cities support them. |
| 🟢 | [Homeownership is Dead! Long Live the Permanent Real Estate Cooperative!](https://www.theselc.org/homeownership_is_dead) (Janelle Orsi, SELC) | The values case for capping equity. Good for the "community over profit" conversation. |
| 🟢 | [Tenancy in Common: What You Need To Know](https://www.chase.com/personal/mortgage/education/buying-a-home/tenancy-in-common) (Chase) | The TIC alternative. Note the risks: each co-owner is liable for the whole debt, and a court can force a sale. |
| 🟡 | [Legal Structures for Intentional Communities](https://www.ic.org/product/legal-structures/) (Foundation for Intentional Community, free ebook) | Seven steps for choosing an entity. |
| 🟡 | [The Limited Equity Cooperative: An Economic and Social Solution](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/poverty-journal/?p=361) (Georgetown Journal on Poverty Law & Policy, 2019) | The economics of the inflation-adjusted resale model, and how a blanket mortgage spreads risk. |
| 🔴 | [Community Land Trusts and Limited Equity Cooperatives](https://www.lincolninst.edu/publications/working-papers/community-land-trusts-limited-equity-cooperatives) (Lincoln Institute, 2014; [PDF](https://www.lincolninst.edu/app/uploads/2024/04/2485_1831_Ehlenz20WP14ME1.pdf)) | Five case studies where a land trust holds the land and backs the co-op's mortgage. |

## Share pricing, resale, and joining and leaving
| | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 🟡 | [Guide to Resale Policies](https://uhab.org/resource/guide-to-resale-policies/) (UHAB) | **The most practical resource for share pricing.** Three model formulas (no profit, 3% simple interest, per room), credit for improvements, flip taxes. |
| 🟡 | [Choosing a Resale Formula](https://burlingtonassociates.com/clt-resources/choosing-a-resale-formula/) (Burlington Associates) | Design trade-offs between formulas based on appraisals, on an index, or on itemized costs. |
| 🟢 | [Housing Cooperative Guide](https://resurrectionproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/TRP-Co-Op-Guide.pdf) (The Resurrection Project, Chicago) | Resale caps, right of first refusal, monthly charges, reserves, governing documents. |
| 🔴 | [Fannie Mae: Loan Eligibility for Co-op Share Loans](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b4-2.3-04/loan-eligibility-co-op-share-loans) | What our bylaws would need for future members to get mainstream share loans. Flip-tax limits apply. |

## Budgets and reserves
| | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | [Stay in Shape: Your Capital Replacement Reserve Plan](https://www.agency.coop/node/996) (Agency for Co-operative Housing) | A plain-language reserve plan: list the building's components, their lifespans and costs, and update every three years. |
| 🔴 | [Reserve Study Standards](https://www.caionline.org/getmedia/d163c0aa-3bfd-45db-a523-39681ccbc1c6/ReserveStudyResourcesJune2026.pdf) (Community Associations Institute) | The industry standard. Its full, threshold and baseline funding goals are the vocabulary to use with a reserve-study firm. |
| 🟡 | [Pro Forma Template](https://institute.coop/resources/pro-forma-template) (Democracy at Work Institute, Excel) | Built for worker co-ops but adaptable. We found no reputable free US template for small housing co-ops. |
| 🟡 | [Capital costs and pro-forma operating budget template](https://centre.support/resources/capital-costs-and-pro-forma-operating-budget-template/) and [Paths for housing co-ops calculator](https://centre.support/resources/paths-for-housing-co-ops-calculator-tool/) (Canada, Excel) | Housing-specific spreadsheets. Swap in US tax and loan assumptions. |

## Tax
| | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 🟡 | [IRS Publication 530: Tax Information for Homeowners](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p530) | How a co-op member calculates their deductible share of the co-op's taxes and mortgage interest. |
| 🔴 | [26 U.S. Code § 216](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/216) | The rule that lets members deduct their share. The co-op needs a single class of stock and must meet one of three tests (80% of income from members, 80% of floor space used by members, or 90% of spending on the property). **Renting units to non-members can break the income test.** |
| 🟢 | [Living in a Housing Cooperative](https://coophousing.org/living-in-a-housing-cooperative/) (NAHC) | The annual deduction statement, the capital-gains exclusion, HO-6 insurance, limits on subletting. |

## Financing
| | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | [Chicago co-op buys 6 units with CDFI financing](https://ofn.org/impact-stories/chicago-co-op-offers-affordable-homes-for-local-families-with-help-of-cdfi-financing) (OFN, 2022) | **The closest precedent to our project:** Pilsen Housing Co-op used member capital plus a Shared Capital Cooperative loan. |
| 🟢 | [Housing Cooperative Financing Guide](https://resurrectionproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Guide-to-Financing-Your-Co-op-Purchase.pdf) (The Resurrection Project) | Share loans vs. blanket financing, and where the money can come from. |
| 🟡 | [Shared Capital Cooperative: Borrow](https://sharedcapital.coop/borrow/) | A national co-op lender (CDFI), $5k–$1M over 1–10 years for housing-co-op purchase and rehab. **The most relevant lender to talk to.** |
| 🟡 | [Chicago Community Loan Fund](https://cclfchicago.org/) and [Center for Shared Ownership](https://cclfchicago.org/center-for-shared-ownership/) | A local lender plus technical help. It lent $600k to [Woolman-Washington](https://cclfchicago.org/housing-cooperative-combats-gentrification-in-wicker-park/). |
| 🟡 | [National Cooperative Bank: Housing](https://ncb.coop/sectors-we-serve/housing) | The national co-op bank. Its typical loans may be larger than ours, so call them. |
| 🟡 | [Capital Impact Partners: Co-ops](https://www.capitalimpact.org/focus/co-ops/) | A co-op-focused lender (CDFI), with occasional grants. |
| 🟡 | [An Introduction to Financing Cooperative Businesses](https://cdn.ofn.org/uploads/2022/01/24093430/intro-to-financing-cooperatives.pdf) (CCLF, PDF) | How lenders evaluate co-ops, and how member money and loans are combined. |
| 🟡 | [Community Capital Raising Strategies](https://geo.coop/content/community-capital-raising-strategies-worker-cooperatives) (Project Equity) | Raising money directly from your community through share or note offerings. Note that **member and community loans can raise securities-law questions**, so ask the lawyer. |
| 🟢 | [5 ways to get a loan when co-buying](https://supernuclear.substack.com/p/5-ways-to-get-a-loan-when-co-buying) (Supernuclear) | The friend-group view of who signs the loan. |
