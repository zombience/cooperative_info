# Chicago and Illinois

Local law, programs, organizations, and data. Our lawyer is the authority on the legal pieces. These links help us ask good questions. Some Illinois statute pages are linked through FindLaw mirrors because the official site blocks some automated access, so the lawyer should confirm against the official ILCS text.

Links checked 2026-10-04. City programs change, so confirm a program is still running before relying on it.

## Start here
| Read | Why |
|---|---|
| [Chicago Housing Cooperatives, Explained](https://www.citybureau.org/newswire/2022/11/2/chicago-housing-cooperatives-explained) (City Bureau / Chicago Reader, 2022) | The best short local primer: zero-equity, limited-equity and market-rate co-ops, and Chicago's co-op history. |
| [Housing cooperatives FAQ](https://www.illinoislegalaid.org/index%2ephp/legal-information/housing-cooperatives) (Illinois Legal Aid Online) | Plain-language Illinois basics: proprietary leases, board approval of sales, rights of first refusal, carrying charges. |
| [Holding the Line: Inside Chicago's Trailblazing Housing Cooperatives](https://eps.edu.miami.edu/_assets/pdf/chicago-field-note-new-generation-housing-cooperatives.pdf) (COLA Lab, University of Miami, 2026) | Case studies of Logan Square Cooperative (8 units), Pilsen Housing Co-op and La Villita. They closed **financing gaps with seller financing, founder bridge loans, local lenders, and city funds**. Very relevant to our "two now, one later" problem. |
| [Housing Cooperative Guide](https://resurrectionproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/TRP-Co-Op-Guide.pdf) (The Resurrection Project, PDF) | A Chicago nonprofit's guide to forming a true co-op corporation: articles of incorporation, bylaws, occupancy agreements, the Section 216 tax rule. |

## Illinois law (orientation only)
| Source | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [Illinois Cooperative Law](https://co-oplaw.org/illinois-cooperative-law/) (Sustainable Economies Law Center) | A survey of every Illinois co-op statute. **Illinois has no dedicated housing co-op statute.** Most housing co-ops use the not-for-profit corporation act. |
| [805 ILCS 105/103.05](https://codes.findlaw.com/il/chapter-805-business-organizations/il-st-sect-805-105-103-05/) (General Not For Profit Corporation Act, permitted purposes) | Expressly allows "ownership or administration of residential property on a cooperative basis." This is the usual vehicle for an Illinois co-op corporation. |
| [805 ILCS 317, Limited Worker Cooperative Association Act](https://witnessslips.ilga.gov/ftp/ILCS/Ch%200805/Act%200317/080503170F.html) | A 2020 law for worker and multi-stakeholder co-ops. Generally **not** the right vehicle for a resident housing co-op, but you'll see it mentioned. |
| [Common Interest Community Association Act, 765 ILCS 160](https://ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs5.asp?ActID=3273) | Explicitly does **not** cover co-ops. Useful for knowing which rules don't apply to us. |
| [Condominium Property Act definitions, 765 ILCS 605/2](https://ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076506050K2.htm) | Only relevant if we consider a condo structure instead. |

## Property taxes
Chicago property taxes are high, so taxes are a major budget line and a major risk.

| Source | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [35 ILCS 200/15-175, General homestead exemption](https://ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/003502000K15-175.htm) | Co-op units occupied by shareholders qualify. **The co-op must pass each member's savings through to that member only.** Willfully failing to do so is a misdemeanor. |
| [Homeowner Exemption](https://www.cookcountyassessoril.gov/homeowner-exemption) (Cook County Assessor) | Reduces the taxable value by $10,000 per qualifying unit. **Co-ops can't file online.** The co-op files a separate form by email (assessor.coops@cookcountyil.gov) or mail. |
| [Co-op Homeowner Exemption application (2024)](https://prodassets.cookcountyassessoril.gov/s3fs-public/event/coopHomeownerExemption-2024_application_fillable_20250314.pdf) | One form per property tax parcel (PIN), filed by a co-op officer. It lists units, shares, and which units are occupied as principal residences on January 1. |
| [All Cook County exemptions](https://www.cookcountyassessoril.gov/exemptions) | Senior, senior freeze, disability, veteran, and other exemptions. Ask the lawyer how these flow through a co-op. |
| [Property classification codes](https://prodassets.cookcountyassessoril.gov/s3fs-public/form_documents/Class_codes_definitions_12.16.24.pdf) | Cook County's property classes. Co-ops are Class 2-13, assessed at 10% of market value. |
| [Appeals](https://www.cookcountyassessoril.gov/appeals) (Assessor), [Board of Review](https://www.cookcountyboardofreview.com/), [Treasurer](https://www.cookcountytreasurer.com/) | How to appeal an assessment (free, with short deadlines by township) and how bills are paid (two installments a year). |

## Transfer taxes
| Source | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [Real Property Transfer Tax](https://chicago.gov/city/en/depts/fin/supp_info/revenue/tax_list/real_property_transfertax.html) (City of Chicago) | $5.25 per $500 of price: $3.75 paid by the buyer plus $1.50 (the CTA portion) paid by the seller. |
| [Transfer Tax Ruling #1: Real Estate Cooperatives](https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/rev/supp_info/TaxRulingsandRegulations/RPTTRuling1.pdf) | **Every co-op share transfer is taxable, even a single share.** This affects every future buy-in and buyout, so build it into the resale math. |
| [Public Act 93-0657](https://ilga.gov/ftp/Public%20Acts/93/093-0657.htm) | Illinois' state transfer tax on transfers of a "controlling interest" (over 50%) in an entity that owns real estate. Ask the lawyer whether it matters for us. |

## Landlord-tenant
| Source | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [Chicago Residential Landlord and Tenant Ordinance](https://www.tenants-rights.org/?p=420) (text reposted by the Metropolitan Tenants Organization) | Co-op units occupied only by shareholders are excluded from the ordinance. **If we rent a unit to a non-member** (for example, while waiting for the later household, or a fourth unit in a 4-flat), the ordinance probably applies to that unit. |

## Local organizations, lenders, and programs
| Organization | Why |
|---|---|
| [Chicago Community Loan Fund](https://cclfchicago.org/) | A local community development lender (CDFI) that has lent to Chicago limited-equity co-ops. A likely financing conversation. |
| [Center for Shared Ownership](https://cclfchicago.org/center-for-shared-ownership/) (CCLF + Chicago Rehab Network) | Training and technical help for co-ops on governance and operations. **A good first call.** |
| [Cooperative Communities of Chicago](https://chicagocoop.net/) | CCLF's hub: resources for starting a co-op, a directory, financing info, events. |
| [Chicago Rehab Network](https://www.chicagorehab.org/) | A coalition of community development groups; co-runs the Center for Shared Ownership. |
| [NHS Chicago](https://www.nhschicago.org/) | Homebuyer counseling, lending, repair grants. Ask whether they lend on co-op shares. |
| [Shared Equity Investment Program RFP](https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/doh/general/Coop_RFP_UPDATED.pdf) (Chicago Dept. of Housing, 2023) | City money for **limited-equity** co-ops and land trusts: up to $60K per household with income limits. Market-rate co-ops are not eligible. |
| [Community Wealth Building](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/sites/community-wealth-building/home.html) and [Community WEB](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/sites/community-wealth-building/home/ecosystem.html) (City of Chicago) | Free technical assistance and predevelopment grants, explicitly including limited-equity housing co-ops. |
| [Chicago land trust ordinance](https://chicago.councilmatic.org/legislation/o2025-0018827/) (Dec 2025) | The Chicago Community Land Trust was folded into the Dept. of Housing. Check its current status before planning around it. |
| [Cook County Land Bank Authority](https://www.cookcountylandbank.org/) | A source of vacant or abandoned properties, with buyer programs. |
| [Pilsen Housing Cooperative](https://pilsenhousingcoop.org/) | A local limited-equity co-op with five buildings. [How it financed a 6-unit purchase](https://ofn.org/impact-stories/chicago-co-op-offers-affordable-homes-for-local-families-with-help-of-cdfi-financing) with member money plus a Shared Capital Cooperative loan. **Talk to them.** |
| [Qumbya Housing Cooperative](https://qumbya.com/) | A Hyde Park group-equity co-op running since 1988. Long experience with member turnover. |
| [UChicago Housing Initiative Transactional Clinic](https://civicengagement.uchicago.edu/results/housing-initiative-transactional-clinicsignificant-achievements-for-2024-25) | A law clinic that works on Chicago limited-equity co-op deals. Good for understanding precedents. |
| [Center for Neighborhood Technology](https://cnt.org/) | Housing-plus-transportation cost data and RainReady **basement flood** resources. |
| [Elevated Chicago](https://www.elevatedchicago.org/) | Equitable development near transit. |
