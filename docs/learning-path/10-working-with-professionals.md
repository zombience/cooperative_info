# Step 10: Bringing in the professionals, and closing

## Why it matters for us
We've self-educated so that professional time goes to *checking our thinking* rather than *teaching us basics*. This step pulls together everything we decided and hands it to the right people.

## The team
| Role | What they do | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Co-op attorney** (engaged) | Entity, bylaws, occupancy agreements, subscription agreements, member loan notes, purchase contract review, closing. | Throughout |
| **Financial professional** | Reviews the pro forma, reserves, equity formula, bridging plan, tax position. Ideally a CPA with co-op experience or a co-op development consultant. Fee-only, not commission. | After Steps 4–8 drafts, before signing a purchase contract |
| **Co-op technical assistance** | [Center for Shared Ownership](https://cclfchicago.org/center-for-shared-ownership/), [Community WEB providers](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/sites/community-wealth-building/home/ecosystem.html). Often free. | Early and often |
| **Lender** | Loan terms and requirements for the documents. | Steps 6–10 |
| **Real estate agent** | Ideally one who understands multi-unit and co-op purchases. | When searching |
| **Inspector + reserve study** | Building condition, and the 20-year capital plan. | Under contract |
| **Bookkeeper/CPA** | Annual books, member tax statements, the 1098 or equivalent, the homestead exemption filing. | After closing |

## Packet for the financial reviewer
1. Purpose statement (Step 1)
2. Structure decision and the lawyer's memo (Step 3)
3. Equity model and resale formula (Step 4)
4. Plan for covering the gap until the later household joins, plus exit rules (Step 5)
5. Financing terms (Step 6)
6. Operating budget and reserve plan (Step 7)
7. Tax questions (Step 8)
8. [Questions for a financial professional](../worksheets/questions-for-a-financial-advisor.md)

## Rough order to closing
1. Form the entity and open a bank account.
2. Members sign subscription agreements and pay deposits.
3. Get lender pre-approval or a term sheet.
4. Find a property; offer with inspection and financing contingencies.
5. Inspection, reserve study (or estimate), appraisal.
6. Financial professional's final review.
7. Members fund their equity; close; record share certificates and occupancy agreements.
8. Within the first year: file the homestead exemption, set up books and reserves, hold the first annual meeting.

Confirm this order with the lawyer, who may change it.
