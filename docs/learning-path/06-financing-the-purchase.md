# Step 6: Financing the purchase

## Why it matters for us
Financing decides how much cash members need, what the building costs each month, and who is personally on the hook. Small co-ops have fewer lender options than ordinary homebuyers. Start these conversations early, before picking a property.

## The pieces of the money
1. **Total project cost** = price + closing costs + initial repairs + starting reserves. Radish's renovation added about 40% to the purchase price ([Rent vs Buy vs Build](https://supernuclear.substack.com/p/rent-vs-buy-vs-build-1610219b24b8)), so don't underestimate repairs.
2. **Member equity**: what members put in, from share purchases and possibly member loans.
3. **Blanket mortgage**: one loan to the co-op. Lenders look at loan-to-value (often 75 to 80% maximum) and debt service coverage (the building's income needs to comfortably exceed the mortgage payments).
4. **Gap financing**: seller financing, founder bridge loans, city programs, or grants. The Chicago co-ops in the [COLA Lab study](https://eps.edu.miami.edu/_assets/pdf/chicago-field-note-new-generation-housing-cooperatives.pdf) used all of these.

## Who lends to small co-ops
| Lender | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Shared Capital Cooperative](https://sharedcapital.coop/borrow/) | National co-op lender (CDFI). $5k–$1M, 1–10 year terms. Financed [Pilsen's six-unit building](https://ofn.org/impact-stories/chicago-co-op-offers-affordable-homes-for-local-families-with-help-of-cdfi-financing). |
| [Chicago Community Loan Fund](https://cclfchicago.org/) | Local CDFI that has lent to Chicago co-ops. Also runs the [Center for Shared Ownership](https://cclfchicago.org/center-for-shared-ownership/). |
| [National Cooperative Bank](https://ncb.coop/sectors-we-serve/housing) | The national co-op bank. Ask about the minimum loan size. |
| [Capital Impact Partners](https://www.capitalimpact.org/focus/co-ops/) | Co-op-focused CDFI. |
| Local banks and credit unions | Sometimes, especially for an LLC with personal guarantors. |
| City programs | [Shared Equity Investment Program](https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/doh/general/Coop_RFP_UPDATED.pdf) and [Community Wealth Building](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/sites/community-wealth-building/home.html). Limited-equity co-ops only, with income limits. Check for current rounds. |

Note: many CDFI loans have shorter terms (e.g., 10 years) than a 30-year home mortgage, with a balloon payment or refinance at the end. Plan for that.

## Who signs?
See [5 ways to get a loan when co-buying](https://supernuclear.substack.com/p/5-ways-to-get-a-loan-when-co-buying). Ask every lender whether members must personally guarantee the loan, and what that means if the co-op defaults.

## Discuss
1. What total project cost can we support? Work it backward from what members can put in (snapshots) and what monthly cost we can carry.
2. Who's willing to personally guarantee a loan? On what terms?
3. Who will make the first calls: the Center for Shared Ownership, Shared Capital, and CCLF?

## Decide or record
- A target price range and equity/loan mix.
- A lender shortlist, with notes from each call.
